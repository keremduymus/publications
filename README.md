I am a historian and sociologist working on the global history of the nineteenth century
from the perspective of Africa and West Asia — Ottoman–African relations, the Sahara,
and the Sokoto Caliphate. This repository provides open-access copies of my publications
(where the licence permits), together with abstracts and bibliographic data.

Each publication has its own folder, containing an abstract and — where the licence
permits — the open-access version of the text. Click a **Repository** link below to open
the corresponding folder.
